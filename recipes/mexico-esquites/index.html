Esquites

20 min · 11 ingredients

▶ Cook

Tender corn with crema, crumbled cheese, chili, and lime. Makes 4 servings.

protein
9g
carbs
24g
fats
18g
calories
277kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium-high heat.

  2. 2

    Add the chopped onion and cook until softened.

  3. 3

    Stir in the corn, water, epazote if available, and serrano if using.

  4. 4

    Cover and cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the corn is tender but still has a little bite.

  5. 5

    Season with salt.

  6. 6

    Spoon the corn into serving cups.

  7. 7

    Add crema or mayonnaise and the crumbled cheese.

  8. 8

    Finish with chili powder and lime if using.
