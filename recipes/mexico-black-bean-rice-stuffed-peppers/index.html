Black Bean & Rice Stuffed Peppers

50 min · 10 ingredients

▶ Cook

Sweet bell peppers baked with a filling of black beans, rice, tomato, and oregano. Makes 2 servings.

protein
14g
carbs
70g
fats
9g
calories
415kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 200°C.

  2. 2

    Halve sweet bell peppers lengthwise and remove the seeds.

  3. 3

    Rub the cut peppers with half the oil and place in a baking dish.

  4. 4

    Mix beans, rice, 150 g tomatoes, cumin, oregano, and half the salt.

  5. 5

    Spoon into the four pepper halves.

  6. 6

    Mix remaining tomatoes, oil, and salt and spoon around the peppers.

  7. 7

    Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes.

  8. 8

    Uncover and bake for 10 more minutes, until the peppers are tender and the filling is hot.

  9. 9

    Finish with cilantro and lime juice.

  10. 10

    Serve two pepper halves per person with sauce.
