Cumin Potato & Chickpea Skillet

30 min · 10 ingredients

▶ Cook

Golden potatoes and chickpeas with cumin, coriander, and lemon; a hearty adaptation of jeera aloo. Makes 2 servings.

protein
13g
carbs
65g
fats
10g
calories
390kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Place potatoes in a saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil.

  2. 2

    Simmer for 10–12 minutes until just tender, then drain well.

  3. 3

    Heat oil in a wide skillet over medium heat.

  4. 4

    Add cumin and stir for 30 seconds.

  5. 5

    Add drained potatoes, coriander, turmeric, and salt.

  6. 6

    Cook for 7 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden in places.

  7. 7

    Add chickpeas and 3 tbsp water; cook for 4 minutes until hot.

  8. 8

    Remove from the heat and fold in lemon juice and cilantro.

  9. 9

    Serve in two bowls.
