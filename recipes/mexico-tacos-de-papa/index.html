Tacos de Papa

45 min · 9 ingredients

▶ Cook

Crisp corn tortillas filled with seasoned potato, finished with lettuce, crema, salsa, and crumbled cheese. Makes 6 servings.

protein
3g
carbs
28g
fats
23g
calories
322kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Put the potato cubes in a pot and cover them with water.

  2. 2

    Bring to a boil, then simmer for 20–25 minutes, until soft.

  3. 3

    Drain the potatoes.

  4. 4

    Melt 2 tbsp butter in a small pan over medium heat.

  5. 5

    Add the minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds.

  6. 6

    Mash the potatoes with the garlic butter, cumin, chili powder, and salt.

  7. 7

    Place about ¼ cup filling on each warm tortilla and fold it in half.

  8. 8

    Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat.

  9. 9

    Fry the tacos in batches for about 2 minutes per side, until crisp.

  10. 10

    Drain on paper towels.

  11. 11

    Add lettuce, cotija, crema, cilantro, and salsa to serve.
