Caprese Salad

10 min · 5 ingredients

▶ Cook

Ripe tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, and basil with good olive oil — the colors of the Italian flag on a plate. No cooking required.

protein
18g
carbs
10g
fats
26g
calories
340kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Slice the tomatoes and mozzarella into thick rounds.

  2. 2

    Arrange them in alternating, overlapping slices.

  3. 3

    Tuck basil leaves between the layers.

  4. 4

    Season with flaky salt and pepper, then drizzle generously with olive oil.
