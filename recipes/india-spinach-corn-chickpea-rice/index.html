Spinach, Corn & Chickpea Rice

30 min · 10 ingredients

▶ Cook

Basmati rice folded with spinach, sweet corn, chickpeas, and fragrant cumin. Makes 2 servings.

protein
16g
carbs
78g
fats
10g
calories
470kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Rinse rice and place in a saucepan with 220 ml water and half the salt.

  2. 2

    Bring to a boil, cover, and cook on low for 12 minutes.

  3. 3

    Rest off the heat for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Heat oil in a wide pan.

  5. 5

    Add cumin, ginger, and turmeric and stir for 30 seconds.

  6. 6

    Add chickpeas, corn, and 3 tbsp water. Cook for 4 minutes.

  7. 7

    Add spinach and remaining salt and cook for 3 minutes until wilted.

  8. 8

    Fold in the cooked rice and heat through for 1 minute.

  9. 9

    Divide between two bowls.
