Enfrijoladas

15 min · 4 ingredients

▶ Cook

Soft tortillas wrapped in bean sauce, finished with cream and fresh cheese. Makes 8 servings.

protein
14g
carbs
42g
fats
16g
calories
370kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Pour the prepared bean purée into a saucepan.

  2. 2

    Warm over low heat until gently simmering.

  3. 3

    Heat a dry skillet over medium heat.

  4. 4

    Warm each tortilla for about 30 seconds per side.

  5. 5

    Use tongs to dip each tortilla into the warm bean sauce.

  6. 6

    Fold the coated tortillas and place them on serving plates.

  7. 7

    Spoon over the cream and crumble the queso fresco on top.

  8. 8

    Add avocado slices or chipotle if using.
