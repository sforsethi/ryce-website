Chickpea, Spinach & Tomato Pot

25 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

A hearty tomato stew with chickpeas, spinach, and small potato cubes. Makes 2 servings.

protein
19g
carbs
77g
fats
11g
calories
465kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Put oil, tomatoes, oregano, potatoes, water, and salt in a saucepan and bring to a simmer.

  2. 2

    Partly cover and simmer for 15–18 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the potato cubes are tender.

  3. 3

    Add chickpeas and cook for 4 minutes. Stir in spinach and cook for 2 minutes until wilted.

  4. 4

    Add a little water if needed to loosen the sauce. Divide into two bowls.
