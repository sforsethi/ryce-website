Cucumber Chana & Potato Salad

25 min · 10 ingredients

▶ Cook

Chickpeas, tender potato, cucumber, and tomato with lemon and toasted cumin. Makes 2 servings.

protein
13g
carbs
63g
fats
10g
calories
390kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Cover potatoes with water in a saucepan.

  2. 2

    Bring to a boil and simmer for 12–15 minutes until tender.

  3. 3

    Drain and cool for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Toast ground cumin in a dry small pan over low heat for 20 seconds, then transfer to a bowl.

  5. 5

    Whisk with lemon juice, oil, and salt.

  6. 6

    Add chickpeas, potatoes, cucumber, tomato, and cilantro. Toss gently until coated.

  7. 7

    Divide into two servings and serve warm or at room temperature.
