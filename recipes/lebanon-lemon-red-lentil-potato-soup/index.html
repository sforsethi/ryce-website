Lemon Red Lentil & Potato Soup

40 min · 9 ingredients

▶ Cook

A Lebanese-inspired red lentil soup with potato, carrot, cumin, and a bright lemon finish. Makes 2 servings.

protein
20g
carbs
73g
fats
8g
calories
445kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Heat oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Add carrot and cook for 4 minutes, stirring.

  3. 3

    Add cumin and stir for 20 seconds.

  4. 4

    Add lentils, potatoes, water, and salt.

  5. 5

    Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer partly covered for 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  6. 6

    Check that the potatoes and lentils are tender.

  7. 7

    Mash some potato and lentils against the side of the pan to thicken the soup, leaving plenty of texture.

  8. 8

    Add a little water if needed.

  9. 9

    Remove from the heat and stir in lemon juice and parsley.

  10. 10

    Divide between two bowls.
