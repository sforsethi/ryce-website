Sumac Chickpea & Potato Tray

45 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Roasted potatoes and chickpeas with tangy sumac, parsley, and lemon; a Lebanese-inspired tray meal. Makes 2 servings.

protein
14g
carbs
76g
fats
10g
calories
450kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 220°C.

  2. 2

    Pat the potatoes and carrots dry.

  3. 3

    Toss with oil, salt, and 1 tsp sumac on a large lined baking tray.

  4. 4

    Spread in one layer and roast for 20 minutes.

  5. 5

    Pat chickpeas dry, add them to the tray, and turn the vegetables.

  6. 6

    Roast for another 15–20 minutes, until potatoes are tender and golden and chickpeas are hot.

  7. 7

    Toss with the remaining sumac, lemon juice, and parsley.

  8. 8

    Divide into two servings.
