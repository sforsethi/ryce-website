Aloo Gobi

35 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Potatoes and cauliflower roasted dry with turmeric, cumin, and ginger — golden edges, soft centers, no gravy needed.

protein
8g
carbs
44g
fats
14g
calories
330kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Sizzle cumin seeds in oil, then add ginger.

  2. 2

    Add potatoes and cook 8 minutes, stirring now and then.

  3. 3

    Add cauliflower, turmeric, and coriander; toss to coat.

  4. 4

    Cover and cook on low until tender, about 15 minutes.

  5. 5

    Uncover to crisp the edges, then finish with coriander.
