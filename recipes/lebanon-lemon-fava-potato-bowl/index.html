Lemon Fava & Potato Bowl

30 min · 10 ingredients

▶ Cook

Warm mashed fava beans and tender potatoes topped with cucumber, tomato, and parsley; a ful-inspired bowl. Makes 2 servings.

protein
15g
carbs
63g
fats
8g
calories
385kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Cover potatoes with water in a saucepan.

  2. 2

    Bring to a boil and simmer for 12–15 minutes, until tender, then drain.

  3. 3

    In another saucepan, combine fava beans, 150 ml water, cumin, and salt.

  4. 4

    Simmer for 6 minutes, stirring.

  5. 5

    Mash about half the beans with a fork.

  6. 6

    Fold potatoes into the warm beans.

  7. 7

    Stir in lemon juice and olive oil.

  8. 8

    Divide into two bowls and top with cucumber, tomatoes, and parsley.
