Lamb Kofta

35 min · 7 ingredients

▶ Cook

Juicy spiced lamb skewers with onion, parsley, and warm spices, grilled until charred and served with yogurt sauce.

protein
32g
carbs
8g
fats
30g
calories
440kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Knead the lamb with onion, parsley, and spices until tacky.

  2. 2

    Shape onto skewers in long ovals; chill 15 minutes.

  3. 3

    Grill or pan-sear on high, turning, about 8 minutes total.

  4. 4

    Rest briefly and serve with yogurt and flatbread.
