Tabbouleh

25 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

A Lebanese parsley salad — herbs first, bulgur second — sharpened with lemon and tossed with tomato and mint.

protein
6g
carbs
32g
fats
14g
calories
280kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Soak the bulgur in the tomato juices and half the lemon juice.

  2. 2

    Chop the parsley and mint finely with a sharp knife.

  3. 3

    Toss herbs, tomatoes, spring onions, and bulgur together.

  4. 4

    Dress with olive oil, remaining lemon, and salt just before serving.
