Butter Chicken

50 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Charred marinated chicken folded into a silky tomato-butter sauce with cream and garam masala. The Delhi classic everyone falls for.

protein
38g
carbs
18g
fats
32g

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Marinate the chicken in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and spices for 30 minutes.

  2. 2

    Sear the chicken hard until charred at the edges; set aside.

  3. 3

    Simmer passata with butter and chili powder for 10 minutes.

  4. 4

    Return the chicken, add cream, and simmer 8 more minutes.

  5. 5

    Finish with garam masala and serve with rice or naan.
