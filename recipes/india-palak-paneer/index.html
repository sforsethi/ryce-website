Palak Paneer

40 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Soft paneer cubes in a vibrant spinach gravy scented with cumin, garlic, and a touch of cream. Comfort food in green.

protein
22g
carbs
16g
fats
28g
calories
410kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Blanch the spinach 2 minutes, cool in cold water, and blend smooth.

  2. 2

    Fry cumin seeds, then soften the onion and ginger-garlic paste.

  3. 3

    Add tomatoes and cook until jammy.

  4. 4

    Pour in the spinach purée and simmer 8 minutes.

  5. 5

    Fold in paneer and cream; warm through gently.
