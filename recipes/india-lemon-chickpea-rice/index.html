Lemon Chickpea Rice

25 min · 10 ingredients

▶ Cook

Bright lemon rice with chickpeas, grated carrot, curry leaves, and turmeric. Makes 2 servings.

protein
14g
carbs
84g
fats
10g
calories
490kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Rinse the rice.

  2. 2

    Put it in a saucepan with 250 ml water and half the salt.

  3. 3

    Bring to a boil, cover, and cook on low for 12 minutes.

  4. 4

    Remove from the heat and rest, covered, for 5 minutes.

  5. 5

    Meanwhile, warm the oil in a wide pan.

  6. 6

    Add cumin and curry leaves; stir for 30 seconds.

  7. 7

    Add turmeric and carrot and cook for 3 minutes.

  8. 8

    Add chickpeas, remaining salt, and 2 tbsp water. Cook for 4 minutes until hot.

  9. 9

    Gently fold in cooked rice, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Divide into two servings.
