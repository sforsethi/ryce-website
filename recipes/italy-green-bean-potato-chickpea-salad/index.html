Green Bean, Potato & Chickpea Salad

30 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Warm potatoes and green beans tossed with chickpeas, basil, and lemon. Makes 2 servings.

protein
14g
carbs
71g
fats
10g
calories
430kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Cover potatoes with water in a large saucepan. Bring to a boil and simmer for 10 minutes.

  2. 2

    Add green beans and simmer for another 5–7 minutes, until potatoes and beans are tender. Drain well.

  3. 3

    Whisk lemon juice, oil, and salt in a large bowl. Add warm potatoes, green beans, and chickpeas; toss gently.

  4. 4

    Fold in basil and divide into two servings.
