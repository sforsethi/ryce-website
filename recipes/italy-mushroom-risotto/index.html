Mushroom Risotto

40 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Creamy Arborio rice cooked slowly with stock and stirred through golden mushrooms, Parmesan, and butter. Worth every stir.

protein
16g
carbs
76g
fats
20g
calories

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Sauté the mushrooms in olive oil until golden; set aside.

  2. 2

    Soften the onion, then toast the rice for 2 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add wine, then warm stock a ladle at a time, stirring until absorbed.

  4. 4

    After about 18 minutes, fold in the mushrooms.

  5. 5

    Beat in butter and Parmesan off the heat and rest 2 minutes.
