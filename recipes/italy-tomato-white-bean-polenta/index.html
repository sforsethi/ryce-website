Tomato White Beans over Polenta

25 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Soft polenta topped with tomato-basil white beans. Makes 2 servings.

protein
18g
carbs
82g
fats
9g
calories
480kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    In a saucepan, combine oil, tomatoes, oregano, half the salt, and 100 ml water.

  2. 2

    Simmer over medium-low heat for 10 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add beans and simmer for another 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Stir in half the basil.

  5. 5

    Meanwhile, bring 500 ml water and remaining salt to a boil in another saucepan.

  6. 6

    Whisk in polenta slowly.

  7. 7

    Reduce the heat and stir for 5–8 minutes, or follow the packet, until smooth and tender.

  8. 8

    Divide polenta between two bowls, spoon beans and tomato sauce over it, and finish with the remaining basil.
