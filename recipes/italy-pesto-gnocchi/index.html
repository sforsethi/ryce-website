Pesto Gnocchi

20 min · 7 ingredients

▶ Cook

Pillowy gnocchi tossed in a fresh basil pesto with Parmesan and toasted pine nuts. Twenty minutes, start to finish.

protein
15g
carbs
64g
fats
26g
calories
555kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan until golden.

  2. 2

    Blend basil, most of the pine nuts, Parmesan, garlic, and olive oil into a pesto.

  3. 3

    Boil the gnocchi until they float; reserve some cooking water.

  4. 4

    Toss gnocchi with pesto, loosening with cooking water.

  5. 5

    Top with the remaining pine nuts and extra Parmesan.
