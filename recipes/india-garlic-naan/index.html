Garlic Naan

90 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Soft, blistered flatbread brushed with garlic butter, made in a screaming-hot skillet — no tandoor required.

protein
9g
carbs
52g
fats
12g
calories
360kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Knead flour, yogurt, yeast, sugar, and water into a soft dough.

  2. 2

    Rise 1 hour until doubled.

  3. 3

    Divide and roll into teardrop shapes.

  4. 4

    Cook in a screaming-hot dry skillet until blistered on both sides.

  5. 5

    Brush with garlic butter and scatter with coriander.
