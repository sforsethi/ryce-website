Classic Hummus

15 min · 7 ingredients

▶ Cook

Silky-smooth chickpea dip with tahini, lemon, and garlic, pooled with olive oil. The secret is blending longer than feels reasonable.

protein
10g
carbs
28g
fats
18g
calories
310kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Blend chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and cumin.

  2. 2

    Stream in reserved chickpea liquid until silky, blending 2–3 minutes.

  3. 3

    Season with salt and adjust lemon.

  4. 4

    Swirl onto a plate, pool with olive oil, and dust with cumin.
