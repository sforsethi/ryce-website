Falafel

45 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Crisp-shelled, herb-green chickpea fritters. Made from soaked (never canned) chickpeas for that essential crumbly-light interior.

protein
14g
carbs
40g
fats
20g
calories
400kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Pulse soaked chickpeas with onion, garlic, herbs, and spices to a coarse rubble.

  2. 2

    Chill the mix 30 minutes, then stir in baking soda.

  3. 3

    Shape into walnut-sized balls.

  4. 4

    Fry at 175°C until deeply browned, about 4 minutes.

  5. 5

    Serve in pita with tahini sauce and pickles.
