Baklava

75 min · 7 ingredients

▶ Cook

Layers of crisp filo, butter, and spiced walnuts soaked in citrus-honey syrup. Patience with the brushing pays off in shatter.

protein
7g
carbs
48g
fats
26g
calories
450kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Toss the walnuts with cinnamon.

  2. 2

    Layer half the filo in a buttered tin, brushing every sheet.

  3. 3

    Spread the nuts, then layer the remaining filo the same way.

  4. 4

    Cut into diamonds and bake at 170°C for 40 minutes until golden.

  5. 5

    Boil honey, sugar, lemon, and water into a syrup.

  6. 6

    Pour over the hot baklava and let it soak.
