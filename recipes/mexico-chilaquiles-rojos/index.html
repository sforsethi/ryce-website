Chilaquiles Rojos

40 min · 13 ingredients

▶ Cook

Red-salsa tortillas with crema, queso fresco, onion, and cilantro. Makes 4 servings.

protein
23g
carbs
49g
fats
31g
calories
552kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    To make the salsa, put the tomatoes, jalapeño or serrano, garlic, and ⅛ onion in a saucepan and cover with water.

  2. 2

    Bring to a simmer and cook for 15 minutes.

  3. 3

    Transfer the vegetables to a blender with ½ cup of the cooking water.

  4. 4

    Blend into a smooth salsa, following your blender’s instructions for hot liquids, then season with salt.

  5. 5

    Reserve 1 tbsp of the oil for the salsa and heat the rest in a skillet.

  6. 6

    Fry the tortilla wedges in batches until crisp.

  7. 7

    Drain the wedges on paper towels.

  8. 8

    Heat the reserved 1 tbsp oil in a wide pan.

  9. 9

    Add 2 cups of the salsa and cook for 5 minutes.

  10. 10

    Add the epazote, tortilla wedges, and cooked chicken if using.

  11. 11

    Toss gently and cook for 2 minutes.

  12. 12

    Serve with crema, queso fresco, onion rings, and cilantro.
