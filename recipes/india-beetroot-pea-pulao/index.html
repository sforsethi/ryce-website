Beetroot & Pea Pulao

35 min · 10 ingredients

▶ Cook

Ruby-colored rice with tender beetroot, peas, and chickpeas, scented with cinnamon and cumin. Makes 2 servings.

protein
16g
carbs
88g
fats
10g
calories
500kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Rinse the rice well.

  2. 2

    Heat oil in a lidded saucepan over medium heat.

  3. 3

    Add cumin, cinnamon, and ginger; stir for 30 seconds.

  4. 4

    Add grated beetroot and cook for 4 minutes, stirring.

  5. 5

    Add rice, chickpeas, peas, water, and salt.

  6. 6

    Bring to a boil, cover, reduce heat to low, and cook for 15 minutes.

  7. 7

    Check that the rice is tender; if needed, add 2 tbsp water and cook for another 3 minutes.

  8. 8

    Remove from the heat and rest, covered, for 5 minutes.

  9. 9

    Remove the cinnamon stick, fluff gently, and divide into two servings.
