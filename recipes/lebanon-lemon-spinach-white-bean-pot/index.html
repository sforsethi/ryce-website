Lemon Spinach & White Bean Pot

30 min · 9 ingredients

▶ Cook

White beans and spinach simmered with small potato cubes, coriander, and lemon. Makes 2 servings.

protein
18g
carbs
63g
fats
8g
calories
395kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Warm oil in a saucepan over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Add coriander and stir for 20 seconds.

  3. 3

    Add potatoes, water, and salt.

  4. 4

    Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15–18 minutes, until potato cubes are tender.

  5. 5

    Add beans and spinach.

  6. 6

    Simmer uncovered for 5 minutes, stirring until spinach has wilted and the beans are hot.

  7. 7

    Mash a few beans to thicken the liquid.

  8. 8

    Stir in lemon juice and cilantro, and divide into two bowls.
