Zucchini, Corn & Pinto Calabacitas

25 min · 10 ingredients

▶ Cook

A mild calabacitas-inspired skillet with zucchini, sweet corn, pinto beans, and tomato. Makes 2 servings.

protein
19g
carbs
67g
fats
9g
calories
415kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Add zucchini and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  3. 3

    Add tomatoes, corn, cumin, salt, and water.

  4. 4

    Cover and simmer for 7 minutes, until zucchini is tender.

  5. 5

    Stir in pinto beans and cook uncovered for 4–5 minutes, until hot and excess liquid has reduced.

  6. 6

    Finish with lime juice and cilantro and divide into two servings.
