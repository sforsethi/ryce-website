Zucchini & Basil White Bean Rice

30 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

A creamy-textured rice pot with zucchini, mashed white beans, lemon, and basil. Makes 2 servings.

protein
15g
carbs
81g
fats
8g
calories
460kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Mash half the beans with 3 tbsp of the hot water.

  2. 2

    Keep the remaining water hot in a small saucepan.

  3. 3

    Heat oil in a wide saucepan.

  4. 4

    Add zucchini and cook for 4 minutes.

  5. 5

    Add rice and stir for 1 minute.

  6. 6

    Add salt and 200 ml hot water.

  7. 7

    Simmer, stirring frequently, adding the remaining water a ladle at a time as the rice absorbs it.

  8. 8

    Cook for 18–22 minutes until the rice is tender; use extra water if needed.

  9. 9

    Stir in mashed and whole beans and cook for 2 minutes.

  10. 10

    Fold in lemon juice and basil.

  11. 11

    Divide into two servings.
