Rosemary Focaccia

150 min · 7 ingredients

▶ Cook

Golden, dimpled focaccia with rosemary and flaky salt, crisp outside and airy inside. Most of the time is the dough doing its own thing.

protein
9g
carbs
58g
fats
14g
calories
400kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Mix flour, yeast, salt, and water into a wet dough; rest 15 minutes.

  2. 2

    Fold the dough a few times, then rise 1 hour until doubled.

  3. 3

    Stretch into an oiled pan and rise another 30 minutes.

  4. 4

    Dimple with oiled fingers, scatter rosemary and flaky salt.

  5. 5

    Bake at 220°C for 22–25 minutes until deep golden.
