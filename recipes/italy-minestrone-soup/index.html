Minestrone Soup

45 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

A hearty vegetable soup with beans and small pasta, built on a slow-cooked soffritto. Endlessly adaptable to what's in the fridge.

protein
13g
carbs
52g
fats
10g
calories
350kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Sweat the onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil for 8 minutes.

  2. 2

    Add garlic, then tomatoes and stock, and simmer 15 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add beans, courgette, and pasta; cook until the pasta is tender.

  4. 4

    Season and serve with a drizzle of olive oil.
