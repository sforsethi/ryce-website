Chana Masala

40 min · 8 ingredients

▶ Cook

Chickpeas simmered in a punchy onion-tomato masala with cumin, coriander, and a squeeze of lemon. Hearty, vegan, and pantry-friendly.

protein
16g
carbs
54g
fats
12g
calories
390kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Brown the onions in oil until deeply golden.

  2. 2

    Add ginger-garlic paste and ground spices for 1 minute.

  3. 3

    Add tomatoes and cook down into a thick masala.

  4. 4

    Stir in chickpeas with a cup of water; simmer 15 minutes.

  5. 5

    Finish with garam masala and lemon juice.
