Arroz Rojo

30 min · 11 ingredients

▶ Cook

Fluffy tomato rice with carrots, peas, corn, and pickled jalapeño. Makes 8 servings.

protein
5g
carbs
43g

6g

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Blend the tomatoes, chopped onion, garlic, and salt until smooth.

  2. 2

    Strain the tomato mixture into a measuring jug.

  3. 3

    Measure enough broth so the tomato mixture and broth together make 4 cups; keep them separate for now.

  4. 4

    Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat.

  5. 5

    Add the rice and stir for 3–4 minutes, until lightly toasted.

  6. 6

    Add the tomato mixture and cook for 3 minutes.

  7. 7

    Pour in the measured broth.

  8. 8

    Add parsley, pickled jalapeños, and any optional vegetables you are using.

  9. 9

    Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook over low heat for about 15 minutes, until the rice is tender.

  10. 10

    If the rice is still firm but the liquid is gone, add 2 tbsp water and cook covered for 2 more minutes.

  11. 11

    Remove from the heat and rest, covered, for 5 minutes.

  12. 12

    Fluff the rice gently before serving.
