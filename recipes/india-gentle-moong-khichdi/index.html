Gentle Moong Dal Khichdi

40 min · 10 ingredients

▶ Cook

A soft one-pot meal of rice, yellow lentils, carrots, and peas, seasoned with ginger and cumin. Makes 2 servings.

protein
18g
carbs
80g
fats
8g
calories
465kcal

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Rinse the rice and lentils separately until the water is mostly clear.

  2. 2

    Dice the carrot into 1 cm pieces.

  3. 3

    Warm the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.

  4. 4

    Add cumin and stir for 30 seconds.

  5. 5

    Add ginger and turmeric and stir for another 20 seconds.

  6. 6

    Add rice, lentils, carrot, salt, and 900 ml water.

  7. 7

    Bring to a boil, then partly cover and simmer gently for 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  8. 8

    Add the peas and simmer for 5–8 minutes, until the rice and lentils are very soft.

  9. 9

    Add a little water if needed for a spoonable consistency.

  10. 10

    Divide between two bowls.
